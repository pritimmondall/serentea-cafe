## Brand & Style

This design system reflects an artisanal café and boutique lifestyle sanctuary rooted in slow living, tea ceremonies, micro-lot coffee curation, and bespoke craft. It bridges the tangible warmth of a quaint green-framed storefront with high-end, editorial digital touchpoints.

### Core Character & Atmosphere
- **Atmosphere:** Contemplative, fragrant, tactile, and rooted in leisure. It evokes the sensory delight of steam rising over cast iron kettles, linen curtains, woven wicker, and lime-washed stone.
- **Target Audience:** Connoisseurs of fine tea and specialty roasts, patrons of quiet luxury, literary creatives, and individuals seeking mindful pause from high-velocity urban life.
- **Visual Style:** Editorial Warm Minimalist with tactile skeuomorphic accents. The design embraces expansive breathing room, asymmetrical photo collages, subtle hairline borders, and gentle physical warmth over sterile tech uniformity.

## Colors

The color palette directly references the physical café architecture: architectural timber green, roasted earthen pigments, and sunlit lime-washed masonry.

- **Primary (`#263D32` - Forest Timber):** The grounding deep botanical green derived from window frames and corrugated awnings. Used for primary typography, authoritative actions, navigation mastheads, and high-emphasis surfaces.
- **Secondary (`#B96F50` - Terracotta Craft):** Warm clay accent referencing rustic earthenware and warm chalk accents. Applied to call-to-actions, highlight badges, and promotional callouts.
- **Tertiary (`#A5AD96` - Muted Sage):** A tranquil botanical middle-tone for soft chips, subtle borders, inactive indicators, and tinted backdrop panels.
- **Neutral Surface (`#F6F2E9` - Warm Linen Ivory):** The primary canvas color, replacing cold digital white with the tactile texture of unbleached parchment and weathered stone.
- **Canvas Pristine (`#FFFDF8` - Warm Milk Foam):** Elevated card surfaces and popover containers providing subtle separation from the ivory floor.
- **Espresso Dark (`#3B2923`):** Deep roasted umber reserved for high-contrast sub-copy, line dividers, and deep iconography, preventing harsh black contrasts.

## Typography

The type scale orchestrates a tension between the literary grace of `Playfair Display` and the functional, warm clarity of `Plus Jakarta Sans`.

- **Editorial Headings (`Playfair Display`):** Carries romantic high-contrast serifs reminiscent of botanical journals, tasting cards, and luxury culinary periodicals. It should feature generous line heights to preserve breathing space.
- **Interface & Prose (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Balanced, modern, and open-countered. Keeps dense tasting notes, ingredient breakdowns, checkout metrics, and micro-labels effortless to scan.
- **Letter Spacing:** Headlines utilize tighter tracking for editorial elegance, whereas labels and micro-captions deploy expanded tracking (`0.04em`–`0.08em`) to mirror premium physical signage and gold foil embossing.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts in this design system avoid cramped dashboard patterns in favor of open, asymmetric gallery formats that evoke art books and artisan coffee menus.

### Grid Rhythm & Fluid Structure
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column layout with 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters and generous 48px (`3rem`) page margins. Asymmetric column spans (e.g., 5-column editorial text paired with a 7-column feature photograph) are encouraged.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout with 20px gutters and 32px canvas margins.
- **Mobile (Up to 767px):** 4-column layout with 16px (`1rem`) gutters and 20px (`1.25rem`) safe margin.

### Vertical Cadence & Whitespace
Vertical rhythm relies heavily on `space-lg` and `space-xl` intervals to induce a deliberate, leisurely scrolling tempo. Blocks of text should rarely exceed 65 characters per line to emulate literary formats.

## Elevation & Depth

Rather than relying on intense drop shadows, depth is achieved via layered tactile materiality and sunlight-inspired diffusion.

- **Tonal Stepping:** The baseline foundation rests on Warm Linen Ivory (`#F6F2E9`). Elevated containers and tasting cards sit on Warm Milk Foam (`#FFFDF8`), establishing clean boundaries via color separation rather than heavy shading.
- **Sunlit Ambient Shadow:** When interactive components float or lift (e.g., elevated modal sheets, tasting selection drawers), use an ultra-diffused shadow tinted with forest green and roasted umber: `0px 14px 34px -8px rgba(38, 61, 50, 0.08)`.
- **Botanical Hairline Outlines:** For flat structure, elements utilize whisper-thin borders using 1px `#263D32` at `12%` opacity, referencing classic framed panelling and boutique signage.

## Shapes

The shape system honors handcrafted joinery and natural cut stone. It eschews modern aggressive pill shapes in favor of structured architectural geometry with soft, tactile corners.

- **Base Radius (Soft - `0.25rem` / `4px`):** Used for input fields, micro-chips, and tags, providing a subtly softened rectangular profile reminiscent of printed labels and price tags.
- **Medium Radius (`0.5rem` / `8px`):** Used for cards, lifestyle imagery frames, dialogs, and drawer sheets.
- **Decorative Circular Motif:** Circular geometry is exclusively reserved for storefront badge emblems, botanical stamps, and floating tea-leaf iconography.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid Forest Green (`#263D32`) surface with Warm Milk Foam (`#FFFDF8`) typography, subtle 4px rounded corners, and generous horizontal padding (`1.5rem`). Hover state shifts to a rich dark roasted tone with slight vertical lift.
- **Secondary (Terracotta):** Filled `#B96F50` button reserved for instant transactional triggers like "Reserve Table" or "Order Roast Batch."
- **Outline / Ghost:** 1px hairline border (`rgba(38, 61, 50, 0.25)`), text in `#263D32`. Upon hover, background tints softly with `#A5AD96` at 15% opacity.

### Chips & Filter Tags
- Architectural and compact (`roundedness: 1`).
- Default: `#FFFDF8` background, 1px hairline sage border, label in `#3B2923`.
- Selected: Forest Green (`#263D32`) background with ivory typography and no visible border.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Form Fields:** Crisp rectangular fields (`roundedness: 1`) on `#FFFDF8` with 1px border (`#A5AD96`). Focus state transitions border to `#263D32` with a subtle 2px outer glow (`rgba(38, 61, 50, 0.1)`). Placeholder copy is styled in muted warm umber.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Handcrafted feel with forest green fill and ivory checks; outer contours utilize 1px solid `#263D32`.

### Cards & Tasting Boards
- Styled with Warm Milk Foam (`#FFFDF8`) backgrounds, faint 1px hairline borders (`rgba(38, 61, 50, 0.08)`), and minimal ambient shadow.
- Photography within cards features generous internal framing and 4px corner radii, evoking framed botanical prints.

### Specialty Components (Bespoke Additions)
- **Origin Badge / Stamp:** Circular emblem badge with arched editorial typography, celebrating harvesting estates, roast profiles, and tea flushes.
- **Botanical Hairline Divider:** A 1px ornamental horizontal rule punctuated by a miniature centered leaf or teapot glyph, used to separate long-form tasting notes.
